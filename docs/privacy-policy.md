# Privacy Policy — Colombia App

**Last updated:** 2026-10-08

Colombia App (“the App”) is provided by Dwan for informational and educational purposes about Colombia.

## Data we access

The App does **not** require an account and does **not** collect personal profiles.

When you use the App it may:

- Request **Internet** access to load public content from [api-colombia.com](https://api-colombia.com) (country, presidents, departments, touristic attractions).
- Load **map tiles** via [MapLibre](https://maplibre.org/) from the configured basemap style (currently OpenFreeMap Positron).
- Open an external maps application if you choose **Open in Maps** on an attraction (handled by your device; the App does not transmit your location to our servers).

The App does **not** use advertising SDKs or collect location for analytics.

## Data storage

Content is fetched over the network and held in memory for display. Image responses may be cached on device by the image loader. The App does not sell user data.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer via the GitHub repository: https://github.com/DwanZ/colombiaApp

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the App evolves. The “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
