# Privacy Policy — Colombia App

**Last updated:** 2026-10-08

Colombia App (“the App”) is provided by Dwan for informational and educational purposes about Colombia.

## Data we access

The App does **not** require an account and does **not** collect personal profiles.

When you use the App it may:

- Request **Internet** access to load public content from [api-colombia.com](https://api-colombia.com) (country, presidents, departments, touristic attractions).
- Load **map tiles** from the MapLibre demo/OpenStreetMap-style tile endpoint used by the map feature.
- Open an external maps application if you choose **Open in Maps** on an attraction (handled by your device; the App does not transmit your location to our servers).

The App does **not** use Health Connect, body sensors, advertising SDKs, or Wear OS messaging.

## Data storage

Content is fetched over the network and held in memory for display. The App does not sell user data.

## Contact

For privacy questions about this portfolio app, contact the developer via the GitHub repository: https://github.com/DwanZ/colombiaApp

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the App evolves toward Play Store publication. The “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
